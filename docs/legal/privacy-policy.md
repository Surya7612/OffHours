# OffHours Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 4, 2026_

OffHours helps you spend the hour after work offline. We collect as little as we can to make
that work, we never sell your data, and we don't use it for advertising or tracking.

## What we store

- **Account**: an account ID and the email address Apple shares when you use Sign in with Apple
  (this may be a private relay address).
- **Profile**: the first name you enter, your interests, your daily nudge time and how far you're
  willing to travel.
- **Journal**: activities you finish, how long they took, the place name if one was suggested,
  and anything you write about them.
- **Gatherings**: gatherings you host (title, description, time, place and its coordinates) and
  gatherings you join.
- **Safety**: reports you file and people you block.
- **Notifications**: a push notification token for your device, so we can tell you when someone
  joins a gathering you host.
- **Approximate location, only if you turn on gathering alerts**: your location rounded to about
  1 km, so we can tell you when someone hosts nearby. It's updated when you move and deleted as
  soon as you turn alerts off.

## What we don't store

- **Your precise location.** It's used on your device to find nearby places through Apple Maps
  and is sent to our server only to look up gatherings near you. It is not saved.
- Contacts, photos, health data, or advertising identifiers.

## Who can see what

- Your journal and profile are visible only to you. Nobody else can see your approximate location.
- If you host a gathering, people nearby can see its details and your first name.
- Hosts can see how many people have joined their gathering, and get a notification with the
  first name of each person who joins.
- Reports are visible only to the OffHours team.

## Where data lives

Data is stored with Supabase (database and authentication). Place search runs through Apple Maps,
and notifications are delivered through Apple Push Notification service. None of them receive
anything beyond what's needed to provide those features.

## Deleting your data

In the app, go to **Settings → Delete account**. This permanently deletes your account, profile,
journal, gatherings, RSVPs, notification tokens and any saved location right away. You can also email us and we'll do it for you.

## Children

OffHours isn't intended for people under 17.
