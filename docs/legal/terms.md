# OffHours Terms and Community Rules

_Last updated: October 4, 2026_

By using OffHours you agree to these terms.

## Community rules

OffHours has **zero tolerance** for objectionable content or abusive behavior. When hosting or
joining a gathering, you agree to:

- Meet only in public places.
- Keep titles and descriptions friendly and honest. No sexual, hateful, violent, or harassing
  content, and no spam, advertising, links or requests for money.
- Treat everyone with respect, and leave if anything feels unsafe.
- Not use OffHours if you're under 17.

You can report any gathering and block any host from the gathering's page. We review reports
within 24 hours, remove content that breaks these rules, and remove people who post it.

## Your safety

Gatherings are hosted by other users, not by OffHours. We don't screen hosts or attendees. Use
your judgment, tell someone where you're going, and contact local emergency services if you're
ever in danger.

## Activities

Activity suggestions are general ideas for relaxing and aren't medical advice. Do only what's
comfortable and safe for you.

## Your account

You're responsible for what you post. You can delete your account at any time in Settings. We may
suspend accounts that break these rules.

## Changes

We'll update this page if these terms change. Continuing to use OffHours means you accept the
updated terms.
