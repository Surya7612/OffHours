## Version 1.0

**Promotional text (170 max)**

Reclaim the hour after work. Every evening OffHours suggests one small, phone-free thing to do
and finds a real place nearby to do it.

**Description**

OffHours helps you spend your evenings offline, one small step at a time.

TONIGHT, CHOSEN FOR YOU
Each evening you get one simple thing to do: a sunset walk, reading at a café, a quiet hour in a
park, a letter to a friend. Picks are based on your interests, the time of day, and the weather.
On rainy nights you get something cozy indoors, and outdoor picks tell you when to head out to
catch the sunset.

REAL PLACES NEARBY
Activities that need a place are matched to the nearest real park, café, library, museum or
waterfront, with walking directions in Apple Maps.

PUT THE PHONE DOWN
Start the timer, lock your phone and go. The countdown stays on your Lock Screen, and you get a
gentle notification when time is up. Write one line about how it went.

GATHERINGS WITH NEIGHBORS
Host a small walk, coffee or quiet reading hour at a public place, once or every week. People
nearby can join, and you'll be told when someone does. Every gathering can be reported, and any
host can be blocked.

KEEP IT GOING
Your journal keeps every evening you spent offline. Build a streak, see your minutes add up,
and get a short recap every Sunday. A Home Screen widget shows tonight's pick at a glance.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Sign in with Apple, no passwords. Your journal is visible only to you. Your precise location is
never stored. Delete your account and everything in it from Settings at any time.

**Notes**

OffHours uses Sign in with Apple only, so there's no demo username or password; any Apple ID
works. Location is optional and only used to find nearby places and gatherings.

Gatherings are user-generated. Every gathering has Report and Block options on its page.
Reports notify a moderator immediately by push, and we act on them within 24 hours by removing
the content and banning the user if needed. Hosts must accept the community rules before posting,
and posts are filtered for offensive content and links. Accounts can be deleted in
Settings → Delete account, which also revokes the Sign in with Apple token.
